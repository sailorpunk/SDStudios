A few things you might be wondering.

What does a monthly content partnership cost?

Monthly content partnerships start at $2,999. The right fit depends on the level of content and support your business needs. One-off shoots are available as add-ons for current partners.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Partnerships start with a 3-month commitment, the length of your first 90-day content plan. After that, we continue month to month, and you can cancel any time with 30 days' notice.

Do you post the content for us, or do we handle that?

With Standard, you receive finished, ready-to-post content and handle publishing yourself. Premium includes scheduling and posting three times a week across two social platforms, so more of the ongoing execution is taken care of.

What happens if we don't like a video or photo edit?

Revisions are part of the process, so we can refine the work together. Standard includes two revision rounds per deliverable, and Premium includes three.

Who owns the content, and where can we use it?

You get an ongoing license to use your finished photos and videos across your website, organic social, and paid ads. I keep the copyright and may show the work in my portfolio. Raw footage isn't included. Anything delivered and paid for is yours to keep using, even if the partnership ends.

How far do you travel for shoots?

I'm based in Missoula and travel anywhere. Shoots within 60 miles of Missoula are included. Beyond that, mileage is billed at the IRS standard rate, and lodging is billed at cost for overnight shoots. You'll get the travel quote up front, before anything is booked.
